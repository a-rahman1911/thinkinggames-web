## Who we are

Our website address is https://thinkinggames.com. The Thinking Games Initiative ("we", "us", "our") is a free guide to games that develop mathematical reasoning in children through play. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website.

## What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, submit a form, or provide any personal information directly. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personal details through our website.

However, we use third-party tools that automatically collect certain technical and usage data when you visit our site. This data may include identifiers such as cookies, IP addresses, and device information, which may constitute personal information under applicable privacy laws.

## Third-party tools we use

### Meta Pixel

We use the Meta Pixel, provided by Meta Platforms, Inc., to understand how visitors interact with our website and to measure the effectiveness of our advertising on Facebook and Instagram. The Meta Pixel may collect data such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and actions taken on our site.

- Purpose: Advertising measurement and audience building.
- Data recipient: Meta Platforms, Inc.
- More information: [Meta Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/)

### Google tag (gtag.js)

We use Google's global site tag to measure website traffic and understand how visitors find and use our site. This may include data such as your IP address, device information, browser type, referring pages, and pages visited.

- Purpose: Website analytics and advertising measurement.
- Data recipient: Google LLC.
- More information: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### PostHog

We use PostHog for product analytics to understand how visitors navigate our website. PostHog may collect data such as your IP address, device information, browser type, and pages visited.

- Purpose: Website analytics and user experience improvement.
- Data recipient: PostHog Inc.
- More information: [PostHog Privacy Policy](https://posthog.com/privacy)

## Cookies

All three tools listed above use cookies or similar tracking technologies. Cookies fall into two categories:

- Necessary. When you choose Teachers or Parents, we set a cookie named tg_audience, kept for up to one year, so that we can show you the right page on your next visit. It holds only that choice and does not identify you. If you save games to a list, the list is kept in your browser on your device and is not sent to us.
- Analytics and advertising. These are set by the tools above. They are used only if you accept them in our cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time.

## How we use the information

The data collected through our third-party tools is used to:

- Understand how visitors find and navigate our website
- Measure the performance of our advertising campaigns
- Improve our website content and user experience

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use the data collected to make decisions about individual visitors. The data is used in aggregate to improve our site and our advertising.

## Companies that help us run the website

Our website is hosted by Vercel, and our content and images are delivered by Sanity. To deliver pages and keep the service secure, they process technical data that every web request carries, such as your IP address and browser type.

## Links to other websites

The games we list live on other websites, and the Thinking Challenge opens on mathstarchallenge.org. When you follow a link to another website, that website's own privacy policy applies.

## Children's privacy

Our website is written for teachers and parents. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data retention

We do not store any personal data directly. Retention periods for data collected by third-party tools are governed by those providers' own policies. You can find details via the privacy policy links above. You can also delete cookies from your browser at any time.

## What rights you have over your data

Depending on where you live, you may have specific rights regarding your personal information. Generally, you have the right to:

- Know what personal information is collected about you
- Request deletion of your personal information
- Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information
- Not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights

The third-party tools we use may constitute "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising under the CPRA. You can opt out of this by adjusting your cookie preferences on our site or by using browser-level controls such as the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, which we honor.

## Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal. If your browser sends a GPC signal, we will treat it as a valid opt-out request for the sale or sharing of personal information.
